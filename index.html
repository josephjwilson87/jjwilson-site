Behind the Booth

About

Faith comes first for Joe — before the title, before the production credits. He's a PK, raised by parents who are both pastors, and that upbringing is the foundation everything else is built on. It shapes how he leads, serves, and shows up for the people around him.

He's a husband and a father to three daughters — and one very photogenic dog who's just as much a part of the family.

Joe Wilson Jr. is a production leader with over a decade of experience across live events, hospitality, and AV services. He specializes in leading full production environments — from strategic planning to flawless execution — across corporate conferences, large-scale church productions, and live entertainment experiences.

Joe is known for building high-performing teams that deliver consistent results under pressure. He recruits, trains, and develops both professional and volunteer production teams, creating systems that scale beyond a single event. His approach blends business strategy, technical expertise, and operational leadership — allowing organizations to move from one-off execution to sustainable, repeatable production excellence.

Most production teams can execute. Very few can scale. Joe builds teams that do both.

Outside of the booth, Joe talks candidly about the music industry, motivation, church life, marriage, men's fashion, and more — bringing the same excellence he brings to production to every part of life.
